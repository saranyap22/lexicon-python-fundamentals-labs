Exercise 1
Explain in your own words why the Waterfall method often goes wrong when building software.
Give two reasons.
1. It takes really longer time to show product to customers and Customer expectations may be different from what product shown.
2. We might not have all the requirements to create a product which is prerequisite for waterfall method, which delays actual product development.


Exercise 2
Pick two of the four values in the Agile Manifesto. For each one, give an example of what it could
look like in a real team.
1. People and talking together: It makes real difference when people in a team discuss and take decision. Get powerful ideas.
2. Software that works: Having a real working incremental product is more valueable.
3. Working with the customer: It align teams building product for Customer. Feedback and change is quick.
4. Responding to a change: Agile works in shorter timeline, which enables responding to a change qucik and plan in upcoming sprints.

Exercise 4
Your team works in two-week sprints. In the middle of a sprint, the CEO comes in and wants a new
feature right away. What should happen, according to Scrum?
Product owner adds this feature to product backlog and discus with the teams if changes can be added in current sprint.
If no current stories related to sudden priority, it will be added to top of spring backlog.
